Eat, then grow

October 13, 2026

Yooba starts small. Eating is how that changes. Apples are the food you can take at the beginning. Watermelons and pumpkins are for a Yooba who already has some size. If a piece is too big, leave it and come back later.

Trees hold food up in the branches. Shake one when you are close, let the food come down, and eat it where you have room to grow. A bite next to a trunk is a tight place to get bigger.

Growth is the point of the food. The path does not scale up with you, so the same tree and the same doorway feel different after a few apples. That is the loop: eat what fits, get larger, reach the next thing.
