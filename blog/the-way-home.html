The way home

October 6, 2026

The walk has a direction. Grass, trees, and houses sit under a wide sky, and a plane crosses overhead. You are trying to get home through that world, not clear a score attack.

On a keyboard, move with W A S D or the arrow keys. Jump with Space. Press E when something in front of you can be eaten, shaken, or picked up. A controller uses the left stick to move, the south button to jump, and the west button to interact. On a phone, the game draws controls on the screen.

The camera stays behind Yooba. Face the thing you want, then interact. If you are carrying something, interact again to drop it.
