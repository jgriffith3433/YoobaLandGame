1. Open the beta home page and scroll to **Meet the people**.
2. Tap Yooba — hear a line, see the caption bubble, and watch the portrait bounce; tap again to stop.
3. Tap Yooba once more — a different line plays (not the same as the last one).
4. On a phone (or ~390 px wide), swipe the cast row sideways; the page should not scroll sideways, and cards stay fully visible.
5. Tap Pip or Old Reed while another is speaking — only the new one talks.
