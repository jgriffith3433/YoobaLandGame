1. Open the beta home page and scroll to "Explore Yooba Land" (on a phone, or a 390 px window).
2. Drag the map with one finger / the mouse: it follows right away, even before zooming. Pinch or use +/− to zoom; drag as far as you like, the map always stays partly on screen.
3. Tap a pin: the map glides to it and a card opens (picture, name, hook, Play). Esc or × closes it.
4. Press Play: the game opens. The page never scrolls sideways.
