# Yooba Land: world bible

Yooba Land is a warm, bright, hand-crafted island-and-reach world (`x` from `-900` to `1850`, `z` from `-900` to `1100` in world metres) where a small, round, green creature named **Yooba** wakes inside a cracked glass fence after falling from **The Gust**. Everything in Yooba Land is tangible, gentle, and a little bit round: the grass is painted in warm greens and golds, stones stack like pancakes, lanterns bob in the mist, and every snack makes Yooba a tiny bit rounder and wiser on the eight-chapter **Road Home**.

## Areas

### Road Home Regions (Chapters 1–8)
- **Yooba Town (`yooba-town`, center `[0, 140]`, Chapter 1 — *A Town Full of Yoobas*)**: A cobbled town square ringed by cottage roofs, market stalls, warm street lamps, a fountain, and a ticking clock tower. Just south of the square sits the **Glass Garden** (`[0, 50]`), the ring of tall glass panes where Yooba first lands from the sky.
- **Seedvale Meadows (`seedvale`, center `[0, -300]`, Chapter 2 — *Seeds in the Low Fields*)**: Rolling golden pastures and tilled rows south of town, anchored by Bramley's farmstead, the twin Windmills (`[-500, -250]`), and two major waterways: **Glimmer Lake** to the west and **Willow Water** to the east.
- **Whistling Hills (`whistling-hills`, center `[-400, 100]`, Chapter 3 — *The Whistling Hills*)**: Steep, breeze-combed western ridges where the wind plays hollowed stones like flutes. Fed by **Whistle Brook** and **Bramble Brook**, the hills rise toward Piper's Hut and the northwest forest pockets.
- **Crumblecrown Castle (`crumblecrown`, center `[-500, 850]`, Chapter 4 — *The Crown on the Hill*)**: A mossy, half-tumbled stone fortress perched on the high northwestern hill, complete with a working wooden drawbridge (`[-500, 742]`), bannered towers, and a rocky gorge behind the keep.
- **Mistwood Forest (`mistwood`, center `[100, 700]`, Chapter 5 — *Lanterns in the Mist*)**: A hushed northern canopy of giant redwoods and cedar trunks connected by rope bridges, paper lanterns, and the still waters of **Mistpool** (`[100, 620]`).
- **Sugarsand Coast (`sugarsand`, center `[700, -300]`, Chapter 6 — *The Lighthouse and the Sky*)**: Sun-bleached eastern beaches, tide pools, wooden piers, and the Sugarsand Sea (`[1050, -300]`), rising northward to the cliffside **Skyport** (`[718, 352]`).
- **Frostcap Peak (`frostcap`, center `[850, 950]`, Chapter 7 — *The Way Home*)**: The snow-powdered northeastern mountain above **Icing Lake** (`[680, 780]`), where pine switchbacks climb past Old Hush's hut (`[500, 650]`) to the summit belfry.
- **Licorice Reach (`licorice-reach`, center `[1780, -480]`, Chapter 8 — *The Eighth Orb*)**: An eastern twilight peninsula beyond the **Pale Pier** (`[1240, -880]`) and **The Ink Pool** (`[1520, -620]`), where the sky stays plum-dusk and candy-dark stones climb to Bell Hill.

### The Eight Pocket Biomes (`Biomes.json`)
- **Whispering Forest (`bio-whispering-forest`, `[-740, 530]` in Whistling Hills)**: A quiet grove of blue-green pines and soft moss where the breeze hums low melodies through the leaves (`WhisperingForestAmbience`).
- **Bramble Thicket (`bio-bramble-thicket`, `[-640, 530]` in Whistling Hills)**: A tangled pocket of curved berry briars, fallen logs, and wren-song beside the upper reach of Bramble Brook (`BrambleThicketAmbience`).
- **Mossy Ruins (`bio-mossy-ruins`, `[340, 500]` on the Skyport approach)**: Sun-dappled tumbled columns, carved flagstones, and velvet moss carpets from an older age (`MossyRuinsAmbience`).
- **Flower Meadow (`bio-flower-meadow`, `[440, 500]` beside Skyport)**: A waist-deep patchwork of yellow, coral, and sky-blue wildflowers buzzing with lazy bees (`FlowerMeadowAmbience`).
- **Glowcap Grove (`bio-glowcap-grove`, `[290, 660]` in Mistwood)**: A sheltered hollow of blue-and-gold bioluminescent mushrooms that chime faintly in the mist (`GlowcapGroveAmbience`).
- **Boulder Canyon (`bio-boulder-canyon`, `[-640, 1025]` behind Crumblecrown)**: A red-brown rock slot canyon of balanced boulders and echoing wind drafts (`BoulderCanyonAmbience`).
- **Reed Marsh (`bio-reed-marsh`, `[-510, -570]` southwest of Glimmer Lake)**: Shallow amber flats of cattails, boardwalk planks, and frogs croaking in slow chorus (`ReedMarshAmbience`).
- **Windy Hilltop (`bio-windy-hilltop`, `[-375, 380]` in Whistling Hills)**: The high grassy crest at the headwaters of Whistle Brook where standing stones catch the upland gusts (`WindyHilltopAmbience`).

### Waterways
- **Glimmer Lake (`glimmer-lake`, `[-300, -420]` in Seedvale)**: A clear 200 m × 140 m lake with Old Reed's boathouse and dock, a pushable log raft, Glimmer Island at its center, and the sunken Bubble Hollow (`[-320, -400]`).
- **Willow Water (`willow-water`, `[265, -322]` in eastern Seedvale)**: A chain of seven spring-fed ponds and rills—Frog Pond (with 17 stepping lily pads), Reed Pond, Glow Pond, Slow Run, Willow Brook, Lily Rill, and Willow Falls—shaded by trailing willow boughs.
- **Whistle Brook & Bramble Brook (`whistle-brook` `[-375, 100]`, `bramble-brook` `[-560, 120]`)**: Twin cold streams tumbling south out of the Whistling Hills and Bramble Thicket to feed Glimmer Lake.

## Landmarks

- **The Glass Garden (`[0, 50]`)**: The ring of tall glass panes south of Yooba Town where one cracked pane lets Yooba out into the world.
- **Yooba Town Clock Tower (`[0, 140]`)**: The timber-and-stone bell tower in the middle of Yooba Town Square.
- **Bramley's Farm & The Twin Windmills (`[0, -300]` & `[-500, -250]`)**: The warm red barn, pumpkin rows, and slow-turning wooden sails of Seedvale.
- **Old Reed's Boathouse & Glimmer Island (`[-300, -420]`)**: The lantern-lit dock on Glimmer Lake and the tiny island reached by raft or swimming.
- **Willow Falls & The 17 Lily Pads (`[265, -322]`)**: The stepped cascade and frog-pond crossing at the heart of Willow Water.
- **The Whistle Rocks (`[-650, 250]`)**: Tall perforated crags in the Whistling Hills that sing a three-note chord whenever the west wind blows.
- **The Murmur Oak (`[-757, 502]`)**: The ancient hollow oak in Whispering Forest that hums visitors' names under its breath.
- **The Bramble Arch (`[-648, 522]`)**: A natural gothic archway woven from living blackberry thorns in Bramble Thicket.
- **The Windcaller Stones (`[-392, 386]`)**: A circle of weathered monoliths crowning Windy Hilltop.
- **Crumblecrown Drawbridge & Keep (`[-500, 742]` & `[-500, 850]`)**: The cranked oak bridge and crooked battlements of Crumblecrown Castle.
- **The Stacked Spire (`[-672, 1020]`)**: A pancake-stacked pillar of flat canyon stones balanced at the back of Boulder Canyon.
- **The Heron Post (`[-500, -592]`)**: A solitary weathered timber piling in Reed Marsh where a blue heron balances on one leg.
- **The Oldest Tree & Treehouse Village (`[200, 950]` & `[100, 700]`)**: The lantern-strung heart of Mistwood Forest.
- **The Great Glowcap (`[296, 686]`)**: A cottage-sized glowing mushroom that lights all of Glowcap Grove.
- **The Standing Arch (`[360, 505]`)**: The last upright stone portal in the Mossy Ruins.
- **The Sunspire Sunflower (`[452, 508]`)**: A sunflower taller than a windmill at the center of Flower Meadow that turns to watch Yooba pass.
- **Lumen Lighthouse & Skyport (`[1000, -50]` & `[718, 352]`)**: The striped coastal beacon on Sugarsand Coast and the high cliff dock where sky ships and the Sky Whale berth.
- **The Beacon Bell (`[850, 950]`)**: The bronze summit bell atop Frostcap Peak that rings across the clouds.
- **Pale Pier & The Dark Bell (`[1240, -880]` & `[1780, -480]`)**: The narrow plank landing and the final dusk belfry at the tip of Licorice Reach.

## Who lives here

- **Yooba**: A small, round, bright-green creature with big curious eyes and a permanent rumble in his tummy. Speaks in short, earnest observations (*"I am a little bit hungry."*, *"The big tree is humming. It is saying my name. Quietly."*).
- **The Other Yoobas (Pip, Mallow, Trundle, Clover)**: Round neighbours in Yooba Town who welcome Yooba once he steps past the broken glass pane and point him toward Mayor Plump.
- **Mayor Plump**: The portly, sash-wearing mayor of Yooba Town (`[0, 140]`) who keeps the town clock wound and explains the eight chapters of the Road Home.
- **Bramley**: The unhurried Seedvale farmer (`[0, -300]`) who trades a golden seed for a hand with his scattered carrots and stubborn windmill.
- **Old Reed**: A patient angler on the Glimmer Lake dock (`[-300, -420]`) whose whole philosophy is *"Sit still. The fish don't hurry, and neither does the lake."*
- **Ottie**: A sleek river otter at Glimmer Lake who challenges Yooba to a friendly lap around Glimmer Island.
- **Barnaby**: A round green frog at Willow Water (`[265, -322]`) who insists on counting all seventeen lily pads in order before sharing his pond's prize.
- **Piper**: A whistling goatherd in the Whistling Hills (`[-400, 100]`) whose goats climb the Whistle Rocks whenever the wind changes key.
- **Sir Tin**: A polite, slightly squeaky tin-armored knight guarding the drawbridge of Crumblecrown Castle (`[-500, 742]`).
- **Fern**: The lantern-keeper of Mistwood's Treehouse Village (`[100, 700]`) who guides travellers between the Oldest Tree and Glowcap Grove.
- **Captain Salt & Lumen**: The sea dog at Sugarsand Port (`[700, -300]`) and the keeper of Lumen Lighthouse (`[1000, -50]`) who watches the tide and the Skyport winds.
- **Old Hush**: The soft-spoken caretaker on Frostcap Peak (`[500, 650]`) who sweeps snow from the steps to the Beacon Bell.

## What happens

- **Eating, growing, and glowing**: Every berry, carrot, golden seed, and glowing orb Yooba eats chimes a warm note, fills his belly meter, and nudges him along the Road Home.
- **Landmark discoveries**: Stepping up to each biome's signature landmark (*The Murmur Oak*, *The Bramble Arch*, *The Standing Arch*, *The Sunspire Sunflower*, *The Great Glowcap*, *The Stacked Spire*, *The Heron Post*, *The Windcaller Stones*) triggers Yooba's spoken thought and logs the biome on the world map.
- **Waterway play**: Pushing the log raft across Glimmer Lake, diving into Bubble Hollow, racing Ottie around Glimmer Island, and hopping across Barnaby's seventeen lily pads in Willow Water without splashing.
- **Sound-led exploration**: Each region and pocket biome carries its own continuous ambient sound bed—whispering pine needles, wren-filled brambles, wind flutes, marsh frogs, cavern echoes, surf on Sugarsand, and the low chime of Glowcap Grove—so players hear a biome before its landmark comes into view.

## Story beats

1. **The Gust and the Glass Garden (`main-1-1`, Yooba Town)**: Dropped from the swirling Gust into the Glass Garden, Yooba nudges through the one cracked pane, meets the other Yoobas in Yooba Town Square, and hears from Mayor Plump how the Road Home leads up to the Sky Whale.
2. **Seeds in the Low Fields (`main-2-1`, Seedvale Meadows)**: South in Seedvale, Yooba helps Bramley at the farm, learns to sit still with Old Reed at Glimmer Lake, races Ottie, and hops the lily pads of Willow Water for a golden seed.
3. **The Whistling Hills (`main-3-1`, Whistling Hills)**: Climbing west along Whistle Brook and Bramble Brook, Yooba helps Piper gather stray goats among the Whistle Rocks and explores the Whispering Forest, Bramble Thicket, and Windy Hilltop.
4. **The Crown on the Hill (`main-4-1`, Crumblecrown Castle)**: Across the drawbridge of Crumblecrown Castle, Yooba helps Sir Tin oil the ancient gears and climbs into Boulder Canyon to see the Stacked Spire.
5. **Lanterns in the Mist (`main-5-1`, Mistwood Forest)**: Following Fern's paper lanterns through the northern redwoods and Glowcap Grove, Yooba relights the path to the Oldest Tree.
6. **The Lighthouse and the Sky (`main-6-1`, Sugarsand Coast)**: Down on the eastern shore, Yooba helps Captain Salt and Lumen relight the coastal beacon, then climbs past the Mossy Ruins and Flower Meadow to the Skyport cliffs.
7. **The Way Home (`main-7-1`, Frostcap Peak)**: Guided by Old Hush above Icing Lake, Yooba climbs the snowy switchbacks of Frostcap Peak and rings the Beacon Bell to summon the Sky Whale.
8. **The Eighth Orb (`main-8-1`, Licorice Reach)**: Before departing—or as a twilight postscript across the Pale Pier—Yooba crosses the always-dusk peninsula of Licorice Reach past the Ink Pool to Bell Hill, where the Dark Bell and the final eighth orb wait.
